Design Showcase

The plan, from first entry to review.

Outcome & Status

Built, but not yet proven in use

5 weeks

from the redesign to a working MVP

6 months

the build had stalled before I joined

Delivered

  • I designed the AOP-to-PPMP workflow: the split into levels, item selection, the lock, and combined submission.
  • Two frontend developers and one backend developer built it into a working MVP.

Not yet verified

  • The system hasn't been deployed. Staff still plan in Excel.
  • Testing with planning staff is on hold while the planning process moves to the Budget Office.
  • The fixes for traceability, duplicates, totals and approvals are built into the design but untested in real use.

Reflection

The fix was in the data flow, not the screens

My most useful changes were to how data moved through the plan, not to how the screens looked. Breaking one large requirement into parts that save independently was what unblocked the developers.

It's also the change I expect to matter most to staff, though that still has to be confirmed once they're using it.
